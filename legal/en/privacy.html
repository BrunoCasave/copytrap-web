Effective date: 2026-10-26

1. Operator and scope

DOTNULL Co.,Ltd. (Japanese legal name: 株式会社どっとぬる; "we") explains below how information is handled in connection with COPY TRAP.

  • Company website: DOTNULL
  • Address, representative and other company details: Company information (Japanese)
  • Support and privacy contact: copytrap@dotnull.co.jp

This policy covers the iPhone, Android and Steam versions, the demo, and inquiries sent to us about the game. Advertising and in-app purchase provisions apply to the mobile versions. Stores and operating systems also have their own policies for information they handle independently.

Our coming-soon website at copytrap.dotnull.co.jp is hosted on GitHub Pages. The current page has no analytics tools added by us, advertising tags, inquiry form, or cookie-based tracking. Language selection is reflected in the URL and is not saved in cookies or persistent browser storage. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See About GitHub Pages and the GitHub Privacy Statement. The contact link opens your email application. The coming-soon page does not distribute the game or the completed marketing website. We will review the processing again before publishing the completed website and support pages.

Overview: Progress stays on your device. OS/store age ranges are used to check access to this game, not to choose ads. When advertising or purchase features are available, their providers process information for those features. You can ask about your information or an access decision at copytrap@dotnull.co.jp. The details and regional rights follow below.

There is no separate game account or game-created age or date-of-birth entry form. The game does not sync progress to our own server and has no in-game payment-card entry form. These statements do not mean that stores or SDKs process no information.

Age information from the OS/store: In versions with age integration enabled, we receive age ranges and related requirements from supported operating systems or stores. Their own sharing or verification screens may appear when needed. This feature receives range boundaries and status information; it does not request your exact date of birth, identity documents or OS/store account identifier.

The information is processed on the device to determine prerequisites for using this game. Age ranges and sharing or approval status are not sent to our servers, advertising or purchase SDKs, or analytics, and are not used to select or measure ads. While prerequisites remain unresolved, gameplay, new purchases or the start of related services may be held. A refusal to share or a retrieval error is not treated as confirmation that someone is an adult. This process does not delete progress or cached purchase entitlements.

OS/store providers apply their own terms to account administration, age verification and retained sharing settings. Sharing an age range, consenting to advertising and approving a purchase are separate processes.

Do not send support passwords, verification codes, card numbers, purchase tokens or raw purchase receipts. Remove unrelated personal information from images.

Store changes and restoration of existing purchases: On supported iOS versions, we observe Apple StoreKit change notifications to recheck local access conditions. This notification handler does not read country codes or account IDs, send them to our server, advertising or analytics, or write them to game saves. We do not use our own country allowlist to restrict access merely because the OS is older.

In older-iOS configurations that do not start advertising or new purchases, choosing Restore checks an existing Remove Ads entitlement through Apple’s dedicated restoration path. This may involve communication with Apple or an authentication screen. A verified matching entitlement updates the local ownership record; this path does not start the normal Unity IAP connection or advertising. It does not automatically send Apple's signed transaction text to our server or support mailbox. Cancellation or a retrieval failure alone does not erase previously saved ownership.

3. Mobile advertising and purchases

Differences by OS: iOS 17.0–26.1 follows a compatibility path without a game-created country allowlist or age entry form. Advertising and new purchases are not uniformly disabled solely because the OS is older; they depend on the app version’s configuration, access prerequisites, consent status, products and entitlement checks. Existing ownership is retained and can be restored where prerequisites are met. An OS lacking age information is not treated as confirmation of adulthood.

About ads: The mobile versions use Google AdMob and display ads according to required consent and privacy settings. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location, ad views and interactions, device identifiers and diagnostic information to deliver and personalize ads, measure their effectiveness and prevent fraud. Processing for advertising purposes that has not been permitted does not start; an available alternative ad mode is used. Restricting ads does not eliminate all communication or on-device storage.

Tracking permission on iOS: With your permission through Apple's tracking prompt, information across other companies' apps and websites is used to personalize ads and measure their effectiveness. Other advertising consent choices and refusals still apply. You can keep playing without allowing tracking; an ad mode that suppresses tracking is used. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Advertising consent and Apple's tracking permission are separate choices; one is not treated as permission for the other.

On Android, advertising follows choices managed by the consent SDK and device settings. On both platforms, the game does not supply age information for advertising or add an age entry screen. Ad content is capped at G, which does not establish anyone's age or consent.

Limited ads still involve IP addresses for delivery and local caching of the SDK and ad creatives. Programmatic limited ads may also use fraud-prevention-only cookies or local storage. See Google's explanation of limited ads.

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. After ownership is confirmed, the game does not request new ads. Purchase-status checks and restoration processing continue. Buying Remove Ads does not delete information or stop all other communication.

We update consent status through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and provide its form or privacy-options entry where required by the SDK. A result that no form is required is not treated as age verification or consent to every purpose. Where the app menu displays a privacy-options entry, you can use it to change or withdraw your consent choices. The app does not erase a saved refusal or rewrite it as consent. Refusing consent may still allow limited ads where permitted. Changing settings does not stop all communication needed for delivery. See section 9 for younger players and the purchase description above for stopping ads. Reading this policy, accepting the terms or continuing to play is not blanket consent to advertising or analytics.

The Steam version and PC demo do not start our mobile advertising or in-app purchase services. Steam store purchases and client processing are separate. Where the mobile version offers a free trial range and full-game unlock, the store purchase, entitlement-check and restoration practices in this section also apply to the unlock.

The services below handle the information described in section 2. Processing on our behalf is distinguished from a provider’s own processing.

We do not add game progress, OS/store age ranges or support emails to ad requests. Setting RDP does not make all Google processing worldwide processing on our behalf.

Providers’ own handling is also explained by Google, Apple and Valve/Steam. These links do not replace our own responsibilities to explain and respond.

Where we disclose information to another party, we limit it to what the purpose requires and apply measures required by applicable law, including any necessary consent and oversight of service providers. We may also disclose information to meet legal obligations or valid legal requests.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until the device data is deleted or overwritten. Uninstall behavior depends on the OS and backup settings. We cannot remotely restore or delete local progress. Restoring a purchase is separate from restoring progress.

Age ranges and sharing or approval status received from the OS/store are used for decisions during execution and are not written to the game's save files or settings. The app stops retaining them when it exits, and invalidates earlier decisions on an account change or when another check is needed. This does not delete account information or sharing settings retained independently by the OS/store.

Ordinary support inquiries are deleted within one year after the case is closed. Attached images, videos and similar files are deleted within 90 days after closure, or earlier when no longer needed. If a refund, dispute or legal duty requires retention, we keep only the necessary information separately and delete it when that need ends.

Residual data in backups we control is deleted or overwritten within 30 days after deletion of the primary data. The same deletion policy covers this game's mail in both the support and forwarding mailboxes, sent items, trash, device copies and working copies. If a backup is restored, information due for deletion is removed again.

Our email service includes a backup of received messages covering 14 days. This is not a deadline for erasing all of the provider's logs or internal records. These periods concern support for this game, not store or advertising/purchase SDK records.

Purchase and advertising providers retain information under their terms for the periods needed for entitlement checks, restoration, fraud prevention, service operation and legal recordkeeping. Unity IAP's documentation distinguishes orders and similar records retained until a deletion request from service logs retained for 90 days. The 90-day period does not apply to all transaction or advertising data. If you request deletion, we identify the records and the provider's role before taking the relevant steps.

Google's retention policy distinguishes, by data type, retention until deletion by the user, deletion or anonymization after a specified period, and longer retention for security, fraud prevention, accounting or legal needs. Apple's policy bases retention on collection purposes and legal necessity. These general explanations do not promise that all SDK records for this game are erased after one fixed period. For independently retained provider records, periods depend on the provider's role, purpose and legal retention duties. When you contact us, we identify the relevant records and distinguish information we can handle from matters requiring confirmation from the provider.

Deleting device data does not also delete store transaction records. Legal retention duties or disputes may require some information to be retained under applicable conditions.

6. Security

For now, internal access to the support and forwarding mailboxes is limited to our representative who handles inquiries. We use encrypted connections to the email service and handle only information needed for the inquiry. Inquiry text, attachments and transaction-identification information are not placed in public issue trackers or the game's source repository.

We manage storage locations and deletion deadlines and limit information sent to providers to what is needed. If an incident is suspected, we investigate its cause and impact, take steps to contain it, and notify affected people and authorities where required. Questions about these practices can be sent to the contact above. We do not guarantee the security of every internet connection or device.

7. Processing in other countries

We handle support inquiries in Japan. Google, Apple, Unity, Valve, GitHub and their affiliates or service providers may also handle information outside your country, including in the United States. These services operate across countries, so a company’s registered location does not identify every processing or storage location. Their policies explain international processing and safeguards.

When we entrust or disclose information to an overseas provider, we check applicable requirements concerning the recipient’s duties, purpose restrictions, security, subcontracting and responses to individual requests. We use an applicable basis, such as a system meeting Japanese legal standards where required, or comparable protection through contractual obligations or applicable law where required in Australia or New Zealand. Where consent is needed, we separately explain the relevant recipient, country and safeguards before asking for it. Continued use or reading this policy alone is not that consent.

For details about recipients, processing countries or safeguards concerning our handling, contact copytrap@dotnull.co.jp. For processing independently controlled by a provider, we can also direct you to the relevant contact or published information.

9. Younger users

The game is intended for ages 13 and up and is not offered or marketed to children under 13. It has no game-created age entry form; versions with age integration enabled use the OS/store features described in section 2. Some regions require additional protection for users aged 13–17. General parental permission to play is distinct from legally required consent to data processing. Contact us with concerns about a younger user's information.

When the OS/store requires age sharing and the shared range is below 13, gameplay and new purchases are held. They are also held if the range spans age 13 or otherwise does not establish that the minimum is met. If the OS/store explicitly reports that retrieval is not required, we do not add an age entry request solely for this check. This decision does not delete progress or existing purchase entitlements. Contact the address above with questions about a decision or access restriction.

Age 13 is the game's intended minimum age, not a worldwide age of data-processing consent or adulthood. A range showing age 13 or above is not treated as advertising consent or parental purchase approval. Additional protections are considered separately by region and processing purpose.
